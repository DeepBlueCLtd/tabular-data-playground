What this is

A research artefact for evaluating Frictionless as one data-validation option among several. The strategy is build-it-to-learn-it: forcing a working IDE and an eight-lesson curriculum into existence is itself the research method. Each lesson carries a Notes & Observations section recording what worked, what surprised, and what required a workaround.

The eight lessons

  • Describe a CSV · Write a Schema · Validate & fix
  • Build a Data Package · Dialect & encoding quirks
  • Transform · Inquiry · Publish & consume
