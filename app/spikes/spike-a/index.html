About this page

This page is a research spike (Constitution Principle I). It loads Pyodide from a pinned CDN URL, installs frictionless via micropip, and runs frictionless --version and frictionless validate sample.csv. PASS / FAIL is decided from the captured exit codes and stdout per specs/001-spike-a-pyodide/data-model.md.

No backend, no telemetry. The only network calls are to the pinned Pyodide CDN and the wheels micropip resolves for frictionless.
